01 — The Company
A trusted link in
the national nickel
supply chain.

PT Tambang Nikel Sulawesi was established in 2022, focused on the domestic trade of nickel ore across Indonesia.

From the outset, the company has been committed to becoming a trusted partner in the national nickel supply chain — delivering reliable, high-integrity service from supplier through to smelter — and to actively supporting the government's national downstreaming (hilirisasi) programme.

02 — Vision
To be the number one nickel ore trader in Indonesia — commanding market share and standing as the nation's mainstay for nickel distribution.
Mission — Four commitments
01

Consistent quality

Provide high-quality nickel ore on a consistent basis to meet the needs of industry.

02

Reliable distribution

Build a broad, efficient and dependable distribution network that guarantees on-time delivery and partner satisfaction.

03

Strategic partnership

Forge partnerships with industry players — IUP holders and smelters — and Government, strengthening market position and supporting the growth of the downstream nickel industry.

04

Capable people

Develop professional, competent and high-integrity human resources to support the company's sustainable growth.
