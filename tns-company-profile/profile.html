01 — The Company

PT Tambang Nikel Sulawesi was established in 2022, focused on the domestic trade of nickel ore across Indonesia.

From the outset, the company has been committed to becoming a trusted partner in the national nickel supply chain — delivering reliable, high-integrity service from supplier through to smelter — and to actively supporting the government's national downstreaming (hilirisasi) programme.

07  How We Work
PT Tambang Nikel Sulawesi
07 — Our Operating Model
From mine site
to smelter, in
five steps.

Each cargo follows the same disciplined path — sourced, verified, moved, delivered and settled.

01

Source

Secure nickel ore from licensed IUP suppliers across Sulawesi and Halmahera, matched to smelter demand and grade.

02

Verify

Confirm quantity and quality through independent draft survey and sampling — grade (Ni/Fe), moisture and tonnage — before shipment.

03

Move

Barging and hauling from jetty and stockpile to destination, with logistics coordinated end-to-end by PT TNS.

04

Deliver

Ore delivered to the smelter on the agreed commercial basis, with documentation and chain-of-custody maintained.

05

Settle

Final reconciliation of grade and tonnage, and settlement — closing the loop transparently with every partner.

12 — Pricing Framework
Every price,
to the national
benchmark.

Every sale is anchored to HPM — the government's benchmark price under Kepmen ESDM No. 144/2026. Any invoice can be recomputed by either party from three published inputs:

The certified assay (COA) — Ni, Fe, Co, Cr, moisture.
Ministry reference prices (HMA) — published twice monthly.
Bank Indonesia mid-rate — first working day of the B/L month.

An automated monitor recomputes the benchmark on each publication and briefs partners on price direction.

Exhibit — HPM benchmark formula (per WMT)
HPM = [ %Ni·CF·HMA + %Fe·CF·HMA
        + %Co·CF·HMA + %Cr·CF·HMA ]
        × ( 1 − MC )
CF — published correction factor
HMA — reference price  ·  MC — moisture
Recomputable · transparent · both parties
