AXIOM is one master brand — a branded house, not a house of brands. Everything ships under the single AXIOM mark and the single AXIOM standard. 03·6

The RUO messaging standard

Non-negotiable

Wherever peptides appear, copy states plainly that they are Research Use Only, intended exclusively for in-vitro laboratory and scientific research — never for human or veterinary consumption, diagnosis, or treatment. We describe scientific mechanism, pharmacokinetics and laboratory handling only. We never provide dosing, administration routes, frequencies, cycling, stacking, or medical guidance of any kind. The disclaimer is stated as fact, without apology, and is never buried.

04 / Voice
Verbal Identity

Proof before promise,
then stop talking.

The exacting lab director who is also a coach for the long game — precise, quietly confident, plain-spoken, never hyped.

04·1

Voice principles

01

Proof before promise

Lead with the number, the method, the document. "Every lot analysed by HPLC and mass spectrometry to ≥98% purity."

02

Short declaratives

State it plainly and stop. "One standard, across every product."

03

Quiet confidence

Confident without hype; no superlatives, no fear-selling. "If it carries the AXIOM mark, it has earned it."

04

Honest restraint

Say what we can document, and name the limits openly. "This panel intentionally contains no dosing or usage guidance."

04·3

Grammar & style

RuleStandard
CurrencyIndonesian Rupiah as Rp 1.600.000 — "Rp", a space, periods as thousands separators, no decimals.
UnitsSpace between value and unit: 10 mg, 660 nm, 2–8 °C. Use ≥ for minimums (≥ 98%).
NumeralsTabular numerals in specs, prices and CoA tables. Spell out one through nine in prose; figures for data and measurements.
Capitalisation"AXIOM" is always all caps. Pillars in title case (Peptide Science, Therapy & Recovery). Certificate of Analysis capitalised.
HeadlinesSentence case, not title case. Emphasis carried by a muted colour-shift <em>, never by exclamation.
En-dashUse – for ranges (2–4 working days, 660–850 nm) and as a spaced editorial break ( — ).
PunctuationNo exclamation marks, anywhere. No emojis in body copy.

05 / Logo
The Mark

Summit and cycle,
drawn in one stroke.

The symbol is an abstract "A" peak beside an orbit — performance and longevity in a single monoline gesture. Round-capped strokes, bronze on the near-black canvas.

05·1

Lockups

A · Symbol only
B · Horizontal · symbol + wordmark
C · Stacked
D · Wordmark + subline

The wordmark is drawn artwork, never typed. Crossbar-less A, notched X, wide O ring, sharp M — use the supplied assets (axiom-wordmark-white.png, axiom-logo-white.png) exactly as delivered. The Jost wide-tracked "AXIOM" text style (§07·3) is reserved for UI labels and contexts where the artwork cannot be placed. Prefer lockup B in navigation, A at favicon/avatar sizes, D on documents and heroes.

1× 1×

In partner lockups, AXIOM sits left, a 1 px hairline divider (--line-2) stands between marks at 1× clear space each side, and the partner mark is scaled to matching optical height. The AXIOM mark is never subordinated below 80% of the partner's height.

07·4

Rules

Do
  • — Headlines in Jost 300, sentence case, tight leading.
  • — Micro-labels uppercase, wide-tracked Inter 500 — the brand's most-used detail.
  • — Load only the cast weights: Jost 300/400/500, Inter 300–600.

Don't

The brand's hero texture is an additive-blended bronze particle field — points advected by curl-like noise, drifting upward as a "longevity current", colored #7C4C24 → #E7B173 by depth, gently reactive to the cursor. It runs behind hero content only, always behind a scrim.

Under prefers-reduced-motion, on coarse pointers, and in print/email, the field is replaced by the static fallback: layered bronze radial gradients on canvas with ≤ 4% grain. The cover of this document uses exactly that pairing.

Mandate
