About the company

PT Global Sarana Angkasa (PT GSA) was established in 2006 to seize the opportunities of the global air cargo business. Appointed by both domestic and international carriers, the company has demonstrated its ability to compete effectively within the industry.

Today PT GSA holds General Sales Agent partnerships with several international airlines, selling and managing their cargo capacity across the Indonesian market and its connecting network.

PT Global Sarana Angkasa — Company Profile02  What we do

What we do

A General Sales Agent is the carrier's commercial arm in a market it does not staff itself. PT GSA is that arm in Indonesia.

Appointed by domestic and international airlines, we sell and steer their air cargo capacity — building the customer relationships, optimising the revenue on every flight, and running the operation, technology and settlement behind it.

We are neither the airline nor the freight forwarder; we are the party that connects the two, converting an airline's belly and freighter capacity into booked, documented, revenue-earning cargo across the Indonesian network.

On approach — a partner carrier inbound to a network gateway.
Our role

Appointed by the world's carriers to represent them where it counts — turning capacity into cargo, and cargo into a dependable, well-documented revenue stream.
