01 Profile

PT Global Sarana Angkasa (PT GSA) was established in 2006 to seize the global air cargo business opportunities. Appointed by both domestic and international carriers, we have demonstrated our ability to compete effectively within the industry.

Currently, we have established business partnerships with several international airlines under the GSA.
